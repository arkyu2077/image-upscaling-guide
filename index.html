How these guides are written

Each guide starts from a real file problem (a 900-pixel photo needed for an A2 print, a portal rejecting an upload over 100 KB, a tourist in the background of a travel shot, or a still keyframe that needs 10 seconds of motion) and works through the resolution math and limits before the tool. Where a specific product's numbers are quoted, they are taken from that product's live documentation at the time of writing. Nothing here is sponsored.
